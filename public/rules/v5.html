Tank War v5 · 2 vs 2 with items

Rules

2021 Lab 7 handbook

The game

Two teams of two tanks fight on a 20×20 grid with a few walls. Both teams move at the same time, every turn. Items drop onto the map at random, and the safe zone closes in on a random spot every 16 turns. Destroy both enemy tanks to win.

Each turn, each of your tanks chooses one move: 0 straight, 1 turn left, or 2 turn right. Turning also moves the tank one cell in its new direction. Guns fire automatically, straight ahead, whenever they are ready.

Tanks

Heavy6 life · fires every 3 turns · bullets move 2 cells a turn · 2 damage
Striker4 life · fires every 2 turns · bullets move 3 cells a turn · 2 damage

Each team has one of each. A bullet hits the first enemy tank on any cell it passes this turn, then disappears; bullets pass through your own tanks (no friendly fire) and stop at walls.

The map is point-symmetric; team A starts on the left, team B on the right. Walls stop tanks and bullets. Tanks block each other, and nobody stands still: a tank can't move into a wall, into a cell another tank occupies at the start of the turn, or into a cell another tank is also entering. A blocked tank is pushed one cell back instead (it still turns); it only stays put if the cell behind it is blocked too. The map edge is not a wall: you can drive off it, but that cell is outside the safe zone.

Items

From turn 8, one item drops every 8 turns on a random empty cell inside the safe zone (at most 3 on the map). Drive onto it to take it. Timed effects last 8 turns and can stack with each other.

ItemEffect
Heal+2 life, up to the tank's maximum.
ShieldAbsorbs the next bullet that hits this tank.
Wide shotEach shot fires three parallel bullets (8 turns).
Rapid fireCooldown after firing is one turn shorter (8 turns).
BounceBullets turn back once at a wall or the map edge (8 turns).
SpeedMoves go 2 cells, with the usual blocking rules (8 turns).
JamBoth enemy tanks can't fire for 4 turns.

Safe zone

The safe zone starts as the whole map. Every 16 turns it shrinks by one cell on each side, to a new square that lies somewhere inside the old one. The next zone is announced as soon as the previous shrink happens, so you can get there in time. Every turn a tank ends outside the zone costs it 1 life (shields don't help). After turn 160 there is no safe zone left.

Winning

A team loses when both its tanks are destroyed; if both teams lose their last tank on the same turn, it's a draw. If the game reaches the end of the zone, the result is decided by, in order: more tanks alive, more total life, more bullet damage dealt. Matches are played in pairs with the same random seed and sides swapped, so both players face the same luck.

One turn, in order

  1. All tanks turn and move; blocked tanks are pushed one cell back.
  2. Tanks standing on an item take it.
  3. Ready, unjammed tanks fire.
  4. The turn counter advances; the zone may shrink.
  5. Bullets fly and hit (shields absorb).
  6. Tanks outside the zone lose 1 life.
  7. Tanks at 0 life are destroyed.
  8. Items outside the zone vanish; a new item may drop.
  9. Timed effects count down.

Program input and output

Your program talks to the judge over standard input and output. Everything is given from your point of view: your two tanks first, then the opponent's.

RULES roundLimit=200 itemStart=8 itemEvery=8 ... zoneMode=1 item_heal=1 ...
<W>                          number of walls, then W lines: x y
x y direction type           4 lines: my tank 0, my tank 1, enemy tank 0, enemy tank 1
ZONE x0 y0 size nx0 ny0 nsize

each turn:
  print   m0 m1              your two moves (0 straight, 1 left, 2 right)
  read    o0 o1              the opponent's moves
  read    k, then k lines    items dropped this turn: x y type (0 heal ... 6 jam)
  read    ZONE ...           the current and next safe zone

Everything except item drops and the next zone is deterministic, so your program can simulate the whole game exactly. Read the parameters from the RULES line instead of hard-coding them. You have 1 second per move.
